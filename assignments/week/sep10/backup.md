# Why is backing up important?

Backing up my computer is important because I keep a lot of my schoolwork, programming projects, documents, and personal files on my Mac. If my computer stopped working, got a virus, or I accidentally deleted something, I could lose work that took me a lot of time to complete. As a college student, losing an assignment or programming project right before a deadline could be really stressful and could affect my grades. Having a backup gives me another copy of my important files that I can use if something goes wrong. It also gives me some peace of mind knowing that I do not have to start everything over from scratch.

# Real world consequences applying to you.

1. I could lose an assignment or project that I spent hours working on, which could cause me to miss a deadline.
2. I could lose important programming projects or class files that I need for my classes or future internship opportunities.
3. I could have to spend money or a lot of time trying to recover files that were lost because I did not have a backup.

# Which backup plan will you use?

I will use Time Machine, which is the built-in backup tool on my Mac. I will connect my external backup drive to my Mac and make sure Time Machine is turned on. I will select the drive as my backup disk and let Time Machine create the backup. After it is set up, I will make sure the backup is running correctly and that my important files are being included.

# My plan to establish a backup schedule.

I will back up my computer every Sunday at 6:00 PM. I chose Sunday because it gives me a regular time each week to check my files and make sure my schoolwork from the previous week is backed up. I will back up my class assignments, programming projects, documents, photos, and other important files. I will also check that the backup completed successfully before disconnecting the backup drive.

|---|---|---|
